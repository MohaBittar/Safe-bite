How the AI Works (v4.0)

SafeBite runs a powerful AI engine entirely in your browser. No API keys or internet needed for analysis.

1. Smart Spelling Correction - Fuzzy matching catches misspelled ingredients and food names. Even if you type "chiken" instead of "chicken", we still find it.
2. 200+ Allergen Aliases - Recognizes chemical names (casein=milk, albumin=egg, E220=sulfite)
3. 200+ Food Database - Knows ingredients of common foods from burgers to sushi to Indian curries
4. OCR Text Extraction - Reads ingredient labels from photos using free OCR
5. Hidden Allergen Detection - 30+ patterns flag "may contain", "produced in facility", cross-contamination, emulsifiers, lecithin, rennet, and more
6. Cross-Reactivity Analysis - Warns when foods share proteins with your allergens
7. Safe Food Recommendations - After analysis, suggests safe alternatives based on YOUR specific allergies
8. Research-Backed Tips - Each allergy has medical tips, safe foods list, cross-reactivity info, and research data
9. Risk Scoring - Rates severity: Safe, May Affect, Dangerous, Severe Reaction
10. G6PD Special Logic - Extra warnings for fava beans, sulfites, and known triggers
