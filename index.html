How the AI Works

SafeBite runs a powerful AI engine entirely in your browser. No API keys or internet needed for analysis.

1. 200+ Allergen Aliases - Recognizes chemical names (casein=milk, albumin=egg, E220=sulfite)
2. 150+ Food Database - Knows ingredients of common foods from burgers to sushi
3. OCR Text Extraction - Reads ingredient labels from photos using free OCR
4. Hidden Allergen Detection - Flags "may contain", "produced in facility", cross-contamination
5. Cross-Reactivity Analysis - Warns when foods share proteins with your allergens
6. Risk Scoring - Rates severity: Safe, May Affect, Dangerous, Severe Reaction
7. G6PD Special Logic - Extra warnings for fava beans, sulfites, and known triggers
